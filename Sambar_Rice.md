# Sambar rice
Adapted from [Cooking From Heart](https://cookingfromheart.com/sambar-sadam-pressure-cooker-sambar-rice/)

## Ingredients
### Tempering
- 2 tablespoons ghee/oil
- 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 large or 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- Few fresh curry leaves

### Everything else
- 2 cups rice
- 1 cup toor dhal
- 8 cups water
- 2 cups mixed vegetables
- 1 bag frozen spinach (250g)
- 1 large tin (800 g) of diced tomatoes
- 5-6 garlic cloves or 2 tablespoons garlic paste
- knob of ginger or 1 tablespoon ginger paste
- 2 teaspoons tamarind extract or tamarind cake
- 1 tablespoon sambar powder
- ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
- ½ teaspoon asafoetida
- salt to taste

## Equipment
- pressure cooker
- something to mix with
if using tamarind cake instead of extract:
- kettle
- shallow bowl

## Method
1. Wash and set aside 1 cup rice and ½ cup toor dal.
2. Chop up all the veg and set aside. Keep onion separate from the rest.
3. Set the pressure cooker to "Saute" mode.
4. Add 2 tablespoons ghee, 1 teaspoon mustard seeds, 1 teaspoon cumin seeds, ½ teaspoon asafoetida, and a few fresh curry leaves.
5. Once the mustard seeds start to pop, add onion and fry until golden brown.
6. Add garlic and ginger. Fry 3-4 minutes.
7. Add mixed vegetables, frozen spinach and tomatoes. Cook for 3-4 minutes with the lid on or until spinach defrosts.
8. Add 1 tablespoon sambar powder, ½ teaspoon turmeric, ½ teaspoon asafoetida. Cook until the raw smell of the powder disappears.
9. Add washed rice and dhal. Fry for 3-4 minutes.
10. Add water and tamarind. Mix well.
11. Check salt level and add more if required. The liquid should be slightly too salty. This will ensure the right amount of salt once cooked.
12. Put lid on pressure cooker and set to "Broth/Dhal" mode.
