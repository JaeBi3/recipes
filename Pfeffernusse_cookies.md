# Pfeffernusse cookies
Also known as Peppernut cookies.
Adapted from [Taste.com.au](https://www.taste.com.au/recipes/german-spice-cookies-pfeffernusse/ralgk4jz).

Makes 24 cookies.

## Ingredients
- 125g unsalted butter, softened
- ¾ cup dark brown sugar
- ¼ cup golden syrup
- 1 free range egg
- 2½ cups plain flour
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground allspice
- ¼ tsp ground ginger
- 1 pinch ground white pepper
- ¼ tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 2 cups pure icing sugar for the glaze


## Equipment
- Oven
- 2 x baking trays
- Baking paper
- Large mixing bowl
- Small bowl for glaze
- Stand mixer or electric whisk
- Something to sift with
- Measuring cups and spoons

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 180°C/160°C fan-forced.
2. Line 2 large baking trays with baking paper.
3. Using an electric stand mixer, beat butter, dark brown sugar and golden syrup until fluffy. Add egg and beat until combined.
4. Sift flour, cinnamon, allspice, ginger, white pepper and bicarbonate of soda over butter mixture. Beat on low speed until combined.
5. Using 1 tbs mixture at a time, roll mixture into balls. Place on prepared trays about 4cm apart. Slightly flatten with the palm of your hand. Bake for 12 minutes or until golden and firm to touch. Cool completely on trays.
6. To make the glaze, sift pure icing sugar into a small bowl. Add 1 ½ tbs boiling water and stir until smooth.
7. Dip the top of each cookie, working with one at a time, into the glaze to coat. Allow excess glaze to drain. Place on a wire rack set over a baking tray. Stand for 1 hour or until set.
8. Store in an airtight container before gifting (or keep them all for yourself).
