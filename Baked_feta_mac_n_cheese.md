# Baked feta mac n cheese

Adapted from Woolworths Fresh Ideas magazine July 2025 p.19

## Ingredients
- 500g curls or elbow pasta
- 6 cups of milk (this is definitely too much milk, but it's what the recipe says)
- 2 cups sun dried tomato halves, drained and chopped
- 400g greek-style feta
- 100g grated parmigiano reggiano or parmesan cheese (not the cheese powder kind)
- 240g baby spinach leaves
- black pepper, freshly ground, to taste
- 1 dried chilli, finely chopped (optional)
- parsley leaves, finely chopped, to serve

> [!TIP]
> Getting pre-grated parmigiano reggiano cheese is best as the anti-caking agent helps thicken up the sauce.

## Equipment
- oven
- large baking dish
- something to mix ingredients

## Method
1. Pre-heat oven to 200°C/180°C fan-forced.
2. Add pasta, tomato, milk, freshly ground black pepper, and half the cheese to a large baking dish. Add chilli if using. Mix to combine.
3. Place feta block in the middle of the dish. Bake for 25 minutes or until feta begins to brown.
4. Break up the feta and combine into the rest of the pasta mixture.
5. Add spinach and ½ cup water and stir to combine.
6. Cook for a further 10 minutes or until pasta is cooked.
7. Serve topped with remaining cheese and parsley.
