# Satay Noodles
Adapted from [The Cook Up with Adam Liaw](https://www.sbs.com.au/food/the-cook-up-with-adam-liaw/recipe/satay-chicken-noodles/0igayn8jh)

## Ingredients
- 900 g firm tofu, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- Salt and black pepper
- 4 tablespoons kecap manis, divided evenly
- 10 tbsp vegetable oil
- 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 teaspoons finely chopped ginger
- ½ small cabbage, sliced
- 8 spring onions, thinly sliced
- 1000 g Hokkien-style noodles
- 2 cups bean sprouts
- 4 tablespoons chilli oil crisp, to serve
- 1 bag frozen spinach (250g)
- 1 cup roasted peanuts, roughly chopped, to serve
- Lime wedges, to serve

### For the satay sauce
- 2 teaspoons chicken stock powder
- 1 cup smooth peanut butter
- 400 ml coconut cream
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons dark soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons black vinegar

## Equipment
- large bowl
- small saucepan
- large wok or frying pan
- whisk

## Method
1. In a large bowl, combine the tofu, 1 teaspoon turmeric, 1 teaspoon coriander, 1 teaspoon cumin, a generous pinch of salt and pepper and half the kecap manis (2 tablespoons) (reserve the rest to serve!). Mix well to combine and marinate for 1 hour (or overnight).
2. To make the satay sauce, heat the ingredients with 2 cups water in a small saucepan over medium heat, whisking until smooth and combined and the sugar has dissolved. The sauce should be the consistency of thickened cream – add an extra splash of water if needed.
3. When ready to cook the tofu, heat a large wok or frying pan over high heat. Once hot, add the oil, followed by the marinated tofu, and stir-fry for 1 minute, or until browned.
4. Add the garlic and ginger to the pan and cook for a further minute, or until fragrant.
5. Add the cabbage, spring onion, and bean sprouts to the pan and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes, or until the cabbage wilts.
6. Meanwhile, use a small, sharp knife to poke a few holes in the packet of noodles and microwave for about 3 minutes, or until loosened and warm. Add the noodles to the wok, followed by the satay sauce and toss to combine. Cook for 1-2 minutes, or until the sauce is absorbed and the noodles are cooked.
7. Divide the satay chicken noodles between plates and drizzle with the remaining kecap manis and chilli crisp oil. Top with peanuts and serve with lime wedges on the side.
